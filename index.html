Privacy Policy for PrayMap

Last updated: August 12, 2026

PrayMap ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

Location Data
PrayMap uses your device's location to calculate accurate prayer times for your area and to let you log where you pray on your personal prayer map. Location data is:

  • Stored only on your device
  • Never transmitted to our servers, because we don't operate any servers
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Never linked to your identity or used for advertising

Prayer Logs
When you log a prayer, the app stores the prayer name, time, and location (latitude/longitude, city, country) on your device. This data stays local to your device and, if you enable iCloud sync, is stored in your personal iCloud account under Apple's standard iCloud security.

Purchase Information
If you choose to leave a tip through the app, purchase processing is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store any payment or card information.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not serve ads
  • We do not sell or share data with data brokers or advertisers

Data Storage

All app data (prayer logs, location history, settings) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, and optionally synced through your personal iCloud account if enabled. We do not have access to this data.

Notifications

If you enable prayer time notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification framework. No data is sent to external servers to generate these reminders.

Children's Privacy

PrayMap does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is a general-purpose utility not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
