Frequently Asked Questions

Why does PrayMap need my location?
Location is used to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for where you are. It's also used to place pins on your personal prayer map when you log a prayer. Your location never leaves your device.
How do I change the calculation method?
Go to Stats → tap the gear icon in the top right → Calculation Method, and choose the method used in your region.
Does an exempt day break my streak?
No. Marking a day as exempt (for example during menstruation) protects your streak — it won't count against you, and your streak continues once you resume.
Can I add widgets to my Home Screen or Lock Screen?
Yes. Long press your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap the + button, and search for PrayMap to see all available widget sizes.
Is my prayer data private?
Yes. All data stays on your device. We don't collect analytics, sell data, or show ads. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I request a feature or report a bug?
